Prologue

The Complaint

An email reached the Audit Committee: Project Falcon — the bank's credit card platform overhaul — had gone live eight months late, over budget, with no clear proof of business benefit. Nine months planned. Fourteen actual. Budget: EGP 42 million.

Rania Kamal opened her notebook and booked her first interview.
